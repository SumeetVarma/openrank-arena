Wayfare 42 — Carry-on Travel Backpack

40L · 22"×14"×7.5" · 58.4 oz · 15" laptop · 1000D nylon

Best for

Travelers who want strong organization, multiple carry modes, and a heritage look, on trips up to about a week.

Real trade-offs

  • Excellent zippers (#10 YKK), durable 1000D nylon, three carry modes.
  • Harness padding is modest — works fine under ~25 lbs but not ideal heavier.
  • 15-inch laptop fits but requires diagonal insertion.
  • About 39% of airlines list it as carry-on compliant; check your airline.

Frequently asked

Will it fit a 10-day trip?

Yes, with packing cubes. The 40L capacity is generous and the clamshell-style opening packs like a small suitcase.

Does it carry like a hiking pack?

No — it's designed for short urban travel, not multi-day hiking. The harness is decent but not a full suspension system.
