FAQ

How many days can I travel with the Pro 40L?

You can fit 7–10 days' worth of clothes and accessories in the Pro 40L, depending on your packing style and climate. By doing laundry every 1-2 weeks, you can travel indefinitely with this bag.

Which Voyager Pro 40 backpack should I choose if I’m considering the Pro 40L?

Choose the Pro 40L if you want maximum packing space, comfort, and organization. The Pro is best for digital nomads and travelers taking long international trips where one bag has to do it all.

Is the Pro 40L heavy?

The Pro 40L is heavier than some bags, including Voyager Pro 40's Travel Backpack Lite 40L. However, the Pro's advanced suspension system transfers weight off your shoulders so that it carries lighter than other bags. You'll find that other bags can weigh less but feel heavier because of a simpler suspension system and a minimal hip belt.

Will the Pro 40L fit me?

Yes, the Pro 40L is designed to be one-size-fits-all and features a height-adjustable suspension system that fits the widest possible range of travelers. Check out the video and instructions here to ensure a perfect fit. If you don't love your bag, you can return it unused within 30 days for a full refund.

Is the Pro 40L carry on compliant?

Yes. The Voyager Pro 40 is designed to meet carry on size limits for both US and international airlines. The Pro measures 22 x 14 x 8 inches (55 x 35 x 20 cm) and always fits in the overhead bin.

The dimensions maximize both your packing space and your bag's carry-on-compatibility across airlines. Take one bag as a carry on anywhere in the world.

Is the Pro 40L comfortable when fully packed?

Yes, the Pro 40L is built for comfort with a height-adjustable harness, weight-bearing hip belt, and load lifters. This hiking-style suspension system transfers 80% of your pack's weight from your shoulders to your hips for an easier, more comfortable carry.

What size laptop fits in the Pro 40L?

The Pro 40L can carry a 16-inch laptop and a 12.9-inch tablet in padded, suspended sleeves. The electronics compartment is accessible from the top of the bag, so you can reach it when your bag is in the overhead bin or under the seat in front of you. Both sleeves are suspended from the bottom of the bag to prevent damage.

Will a water bottle fit in the side pocket of the Pro?

Yes, the external water bottle pocket fits bottles up to 3 inches in diameter.

Is there a place to hide an AirTag or GPS tracker?

There's no dedicated tracker pocket, but the bag has several interior pockets where you can tuck one out of sight. Many customers do this.

Who is the Pro 40L right for?

The Pro 40L is the right choice if you're prioritizing comfort on longer trips, more organization, and increased water resistance. The fully adjustable suspension fits nearly any torso length, making it the safer default if you're choosing between the Pro 40L and the Lite. It's our most-popular bag for first-time Voyager Pro 40 buyers.

Great Quality Less Comfort

I upgraded from the 35L Outbreaker to the 40L Pro due to a torn water bottle pocket and a desire for more space. I really like the switch to a single one zip mesh instead of two. The sleeker front design with a side pocket that's perfect for a passport is also great.

However, the padding in the Pro is underwhelming. Despite being marketed as more comfortable, it’s noticeably less comfortable than my Outbreaker—both packed and unpacked. I tested both bags with the same gear, and the difference is clear. The shoulder straps on the Pro feel narrower and have less padding.

I’m also disappointed that the laptop compartment no longer zips down fully. The half-zip makes it harder to organize and access my chargers. Additionally, I recommend changing the water bottle pockets to a mesh material to allow more flexibility.

While I appreciate some of the design updates, the decrease in comfort ultimately makes this bag a letdown compared to the Outbreaker. I attached photos of the new Pro and the back panel and laptop compartment of the Outbreaker to compare.
