The 14L is best for minimalists or compact travel setups—it fits under any seat and packs daily essentials. The 20L expands for longer weekends or gear-heavy trips and accommodates more packing cubes, clothes, and tech. (See comparison chart for full specs.)

The Travel Pack is made from durable, water-resistant tarpaulin (75% Polyester / 25% Nylon) with weather-sealed YKK zippers and reinforced stitching throughout. It’s built to handle rain, spills, and splashes with ease—but we don't recommend fully submersing it. All materials are 100% vegan and crafted for long-lasting performance.

The Travel Pack is our more premium and feature-rich option—built for those who want a bag that works for both everyday use and short trips. It expands up to 50% for extra packing space and includes a TSA-approved laptop compartment that folds flat for easier airport security. The Backpack expands by 30% and is designed primarily for daily carry, making it a great choice for commuters or anyone who doesn’t need the added travel functionality.

The adjustable magnetic side pockets securely fit bottles up to 3.5" in diameter, including most 32 oz insulated bottles. When not in use, the pockets automatically close for a sleek, streamlined profile.

The Travel Pack features 22 thoughtfully designed pockets to keep you organized, including a hidden pocket for cash, an AirTag, or other valuables, plus multiple RFID-safe compartments for added security. It also includes two magnetic water bottle pockets, a retractable key leash for easy access, and a hard-shell sunglasses case with a soft cover that fits perfectly inside its dedicated pocket.
