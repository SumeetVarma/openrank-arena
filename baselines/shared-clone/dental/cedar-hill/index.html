Raising the game with comprehensive care

Cedar Hill Family Dentistry is the best destination to find comprehensive dental care in a modern, relaxing office. From routine cleanings to complex restorative care and everything in between, we have what you and your family need to maintain healthy, confident smiles.

  • Preventive dentistry: We offer preventive dental care designed for patients of all ages. Prevent dental issues with dental cleanings, check-ups, oral cancer exams, mouthguards, and sealants.
  • Pediatric dentistry: We create positive dental experiences for young smiles with gentle care and age-appropriate education. As your best family dentist in Austin, TX, we believe lasting smiles start with pediatric dentistry. Kids care with Nitrous sedation.
  • Cosmetic dentistry: Discover life-changing transformations with our advanced cosmetic dental care, including veneers, teeth whitening, and dental bonding.
  • Restorative dentistry: Restoring missing, decayed, and damaged teeth is challenging, but not impossible. Bring back the health and beauty of your smile with dental fillings, crowns, bridges, and root canals.
  • Invisalign : Straighter teeth are healthier teeth. Correct gapped, crowded, or misaligned teeth with clear aligners designed to fit your lifestyle.
  • TMJ pain treatment: Overcome constant headaches and jaw pain with targeted, non-surgical treatments with Laser.
  • Frenectomy.
  • Laser Assisted Periodontal Therapy- Laser assisted reduction of bacterial level (decontamination) and inflammation
  • Gum Reshaping – laser assisted gingivoplasty as needed restorative procedures and cosmetic work or after Orthodontic Treatment.
  • Bioclear – Black Triangle Closure for closing the spacing in between the teeth.
  • Partials and Denture- prosthetic dentures.
  • Wisdom teeth Removal by the specialist at our office.
  • Implant placement with Sedation with the specialist at our office.

Affordable dental care for everyone

Cost is one of the most common reasons people delay dental care. As we believe everyone deserves a healthy smile, we’ve taken steps to make dental treatment more affordable and accessible.

Here’s how we help:

  • Insurance: We accept insurance from several providers to help minimize your out-of-pocket costs. Our team also assists you with filing claims so you can focus on your oral health, not paperwork.
  • In-house membership plan: If insurance is not an option, take advantage of our dental savings plan. For an affordable one-off cost, members can enjoy preventive services and exclusive discounts on cosmetic and restorative dental services. An in-house membership plan simplifies dental care for individuals and families. Unlike insurance, you enjoy immediate care without exclusions, yearly maximums, deductibles, and pre-authorizations.
  • Dental financing ***Care Credit : Don’t have insurance? Been told your insurance can’t cover your treatment? Insurance limitations don’t mean dental care should wait. Our financing options let you split the cost of larger treatments into manageable, budget-friendly payments. It’s like having a credit card dedicated just to your dental health.
