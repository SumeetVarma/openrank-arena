FAQs: Wayfare 42 Series

The Wayfare 42 Series is Wayfare 42' premium adventure travel system for people who constantly move between work, travel, and the wilderness. The whole line functions as one complete travel kit, from briefcases to rollers. We built each bag with recycled technical fabrics, waterproof zippers, and modular features that make sense whether you're commuting to the office or boarding a flight to Buenos Aires.

The materials are chosen for durability, the pockets are positioned for actual use, and the design works anywhere. Office professional? Check. Backcountry explorer? Also check. World traveler? Big check. It's gear that adapts to any environment.

Wayfare 42 refines everything that worked in our Global Travel line. You receive premium materials, custom metal hardware, and a restrained color palette (rather than our usual bright options). The difference shows in the details: heavier-duty fabrics, smoother zippers, and a quieter aesthetic overall.

Of course, Wayfare 42 still handles daily versatility, like our other lines. But now it also handles board meetings, international terminals, and other situations where your gear makes an impression. Same Wayfare 42 foundation, elevated execution.

So, if Global Travel is your reliable daily driver, Wayfare 42 is the version you take to important places.

 

Dual Ripstop layers two different ripstop patterns into one fabric. The outer face is made of 105D recycled nylon with small woven ripstop threads. Underneath, we laminate an industrial-strength yarn ripstop (look at that diamond pattern) between the face fabric and the TAF coating. Two ripstop layers mean double the tear resistance without extra weight or stiffness. The fabric is flexible and soft while handling serious wear. Plus, it's 100% recycled and certified by Bluesign, Oeko-Tex®, and the Global Recycled Standard (GRS). Basically, we made fabric tougher by making it smarter, not thicker.

Weather-resistant, not waterproof. There's a difference. The Dual Ripstop fabric has high hydrostatic pressure performance and TAF coating, and we use waterproof YKK zippers throughout. Light rain, snow, splashes, damp conditions? Your gear stays dry.

That said, a minor drizzle probably isn't something you're worried about. It's the heavy stuff. Get caught in a torrential downpour in Seattle or accidentally drop the bag in the Thames? You'll want additional protection, like a rain cover or dry bags for electronics.

We designed our Wayfare 42 bags for real weather that real travelers encounter, not laboratory submersion tests. They handle what nature typically throws at you without babying your stuff or making you paranoid about clouds.

Yes. Every bag in the line has a pass-through sleeve that slides over the roller's handle. That was an easy design choice. Look, we've all been there: juggling multiple bags while navigating crowds in busy train stations or airports. We created this system because juggling should be left to the professionals.

Using our system, you can stack your bags, secure them, and roll everything as one unit through airports or train stations. The sleeve holds everything stable, so you're not fighting shifting weight or slipping straps. One hand controls your entire travel kit. It's the kind of feature you don't think about until you're sprinting for a connection, and then you're very glad it exists.
