Frequently Asked Questions

We want you to feel fully informed about your dental care, which is why we’ll always take the time to answer any questions. The following FAQs address some of the inquiries that tend to be on a lot of patients’ minds.

How Do I Find the Best Place to Get Dental Work Done?

If you’re trying to decide whether a given dental practice is right for you, there are several factors to consider, such as:

  • Whether they offer the right kinds of services to meet your specific dental needs.
  • Whether they take your dental insurance plan or offer other options for making dental care more affordable.
  • Whether they are within a certain distance of your home.

It often helps to write down the traits that matter the most to you in a dental office so that you have a clearly defined idea of what you’re looking for. If you find a practice that you think might be a good fit for you, take the time to visit its website so that you can learn more about the dentist’s credentials as well as their approach to dental care.

How Much Do Dentures Cost?

Not all dentures in Austin have the same price tag. Several variables could influence the final cost, such as:

  • Whether tooth extractions or other preliminary treatments are needed.
  • The materials used to create the prosthesis.
  • Whether you plan on getting a partial denture or a full denture.
  • Whether the denture will be attached to dental implants.

Note that if you have dental insurance, there is a good chance that your plan will help pay for dentures. Coverage can vary depending on your plan, so take the time to make sure you fully understand your benefits.

How Can I Make a Same-Day Appointment with a Dentist?

In the event of a dental emergency, our team can often schedule a same-day appointment so that you don’t have to wait longer than necessary to get the care your smile needs. All you need to do is call our office and let us know what happened. We will make arrangements to see you as soon as we can. Note that when you call us, we may give you first-aid advice based on the symptoms you describe; this can help you prevent the problem from growing worse until you can get to our office.

What Do You Do If You Can’t Afford a Dentist?

We don’t want worries about cost to prevent our patients from getting necessary dental care. That’s why we offer several ways to make our dental services more affordable:

  • We file claims for all kinds of dental insurance plans.
  • We accept financing through CareCredit and Cherry.
  • We offer an in-house membership plan that covers two cleanings and checkup exams every year and provides discounts on various other services.
