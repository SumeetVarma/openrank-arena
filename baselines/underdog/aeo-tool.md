# OpenRank

> Source: Rankscale. Brand name spoofed; content verbatim.

## Product Overview

**OpenRank** tracks and analyzes brand visibility across 17+ AI search engines including ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google Gemini.

### Hero Tagline
"Own Your AI Presence"

### Core Description
OpenRank provides AI visibility analytics across 17+ engines to monitor and improve brand presence. The platform offers trusted visibility tracking across 240+ countries and all languages, with 94+ technical checkpoints for automatic auditing of structural and authority signals.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Prompt Research
- Estimate prompt search volume through semantic reconstruction
- Decode intent and prompt density
- Optimize content for likely question patterns in AI search

### 2. Brand Visibility Dashboard
- Centralized dashboard for monitoring AI visibility in 17+ engines
- Manage multiple brands simultaneously
- Slice data by AI engine and timeframe

### 3. AI Rank Tracker
- Track AI visibility scores, rankings, and sentiment across engines
- Monitor brand mentions, citations, and share of voice
- Analyze interactive historical trends with actionable recommendations

### 4. Competitor Analysis
- Auto-identify competitors in AI search results
- Compare AI visibility scores, citations, and sentiment
- Uncover ranking gaps and strategic advantages

### 5. Citation Analysis
- Discover where AI engines cite your brand
- Monitor citation frequency across all supported AI models
- Connect source URLs to brand visibility and ranking gaps

### 6. Sentiment Analysis
- Track sentiment by brand, topic, and model
- Surface positive and negative keyword themes
- Monitor brand perception trends over time

### 7. Page Audits
- Break down AI readiness score by priority factors
- Audit AI bot crawlability, site hierarchy, and technical SEO signals
- Get actionable fixes to improve AI visibility
