## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Find the Best Place to Get Dental Work Done?
Consider factors including service offerings matching your needs, insurance acceptance/affordability options, and proximity to your home.

### How Much Do Dentures Cost?
Costs vary based on extraction needs, materials used, partial versus full denture selection, and implant attachment options.

### How Can I Make a Same-Day Appointment with a Dentist?
Call the office to report dental emergencies. The team can often schedule same-day appointments and may provide first-aid advice based on symptoms described.

### What Do You Do If You Can't Afford a Dentist?
Multiple affordability options exist: insurance claim filing, CareCredit and Cherry financing, and an in-house membership plan offering two annual cleanings/exams plus service discounts.
